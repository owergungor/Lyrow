# Lyrow v4.7 — Immersive Music Welcome Experience

Lyrow v4.7 reimagines the first-run Welcome screen as a cinematic, single-screen **Immersive Music Experience** that immediately shows what Lyrow feels like before you even press play.

## What's New in v4.7

### Immersive Single-Screen Welcome Experience
- **Cinematic Live Preview Hero:** A glowing 4-edge Edge Light aura framing a frosted-glass miniature Lyrow player with animated 5-band audio spectrum bars, dynamic atmospheric lighting, and a highlighted real-time active lyric line.
- **Instant Clarity, Zero Setup Friction:** Single-screen Windows 11 Fluent Design (`MicaBackdrop BaseAlt`, `860×580` DIP, zero scrolling) with the headline **"Your Music Comes to Life."** (*"Müziğin Hayat Buluyor."*), a single concise sentence, and three compact icon cues (**Synced Lyrics**, **Dynamic Visuals**, **Sound-Reactive Glow**).
- **One Unmistakable Primary Action:** High-contrast brand-gradient **Start Listening** (*Dinlemeye Başla*) pill CTA and a quiet **Customize later** (*Daha sonra özelleştir*) option below it.
- **No Forced Choices:** Uses your existing system theme automatically; no audio device, theme, or effect setup required on first launch.
- **Smooth & Accessible:** Light GPU-friendly entrance and ambient pulse animations (respecting `EnableAnimations`), full keyboard support (`Enter` to start, `Esc` to dismiss), full English & Turkish localization, preserved v4.5/v4.6 upgrade migration, and manual reopen from Settings.
